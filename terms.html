1. Service Scope & Platform Intermediary

SHELYNX provides a collaborative shopping platform connecting consumers with independent local representatives ("Agents") and bulk procurement services. SHELYNX acts solely as an independent digital intermediary and logistics aggregation platform.

By creating an account, downloading the iOS App, or placing order requests through an Agent, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.

Key Platform Principle: SHELYNX is an order aggregation software system that enables community shoppers to consolidate individual product requests into discounted international bulk shipments.

2. Ordering, Pricing & Payments

When you submit an order through SHELYNX:

  • Product Selection: You select items from catalogue indexes (including size, color, specifications, and quantity).
  • Agent Verification & Approval: Your chosen local representative checks product availability and approves your order (moving it from WAITING to APPROVED).
  • Pricing & Fees: Total prices reflect item costs plus applicable fees set by your local agent, which may include service fees, delivery fees, custom fees, or weight-based charges (price per kg).
  • Payments: Unless specified otherwise, payments are predominantly collected via Cash on Delivery (COD) directly by your local agent.

3. Local Agent Network & Conduct

Agents operate as independent community partners. Agents are responsible for verifying customer requests, updating order statuses (e.g., PURCHASED, REJECTED), grouping orders into batches, and distributing received parcels to shoppers.

Agents set their own fee structures and agree to maintain professional customer service, safeguard shopper contact details, and use the platform's chat feature responsibly.

4. Cancellations, Damages & Claims

Because items are procured on your behalf by independent agents, the following policy applies:

  • Pre-Purchase Cancellations: Orders may be cancelled without penalty prior to being marked as PURCHASED by your agent. Once purchased, cancellation policies depend on the agent's specific terms.
  • Damaged or Defective Goods: If an item arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, you must submit photo evidence via the app or Support Center within 48 hours of parcel receipt.
  • Refund Settlements: Approved claims are settled directly with your local agent, often as adjustments to your COD balance or coordinated cash refunds within 5–7 business days.
